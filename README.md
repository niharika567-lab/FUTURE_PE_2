# FUTURE_PE_2

# UGC Ad Content Pack – GlowPure

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates the use of AI and prompt engineering
to create a complete UGC advertising content pack for a fictional
skincare brand.

## Product

GlowPure Hydrating Face Moisturizer

## Target Audience

Women aged 18–30, especially college students and young professionals.

## Problem

Customers experience dry and dull-looking skin and need a simple
daily hydration solution.

## Content Created

- 10 UGC hooks
- 5 UGC ad scripts
- 10 CTAs
- 5 Instagram captions
- UGC mockup concepts
- Structured AI prompts

## AI Tool Used

ChatGPT / AI content generation

## Prompt Strategy

The prompts were structured using:

1. Product information
2. Target audience
3. Customer problem
4. Product benefits
5. Brand personality
6. Content format
7. Platform requirements
8. CTA requirements

## Objective

To create UGC advertisements that feel authentic, relatable,
and suitable for Instagram marketing.

## Deliverables

All prompts, generated outputs and creative concepts are included
in this repository.
