🎬 UGC Ad Content Pack – GlowPure

📌 Project Overview

This project is a complete UGC (User-Generated Content) advertising campaign created for a fictional skincare brand called GlowPure.

The project demonstrates how AI and Prompt Engineering can be used to develop social media advertising content that is engaging, relatable, and suitable for modern digital marketing.

The campaign was designed for Instagram Reels and focuses on creating authentic-looking content that connects with the target audience through relatable problems, product demonstrations, and clear calls to action.

---

🧴 Product

Brand: GlowPure
Product: GlowPure Hydrating Face Moisturizer
Category: Skincare

GlowPure is positioned as a youthful and approachable skincare brand offering simple everyday skincare solutions.

---

🎯 Target Audience

The campaign targets:

- Women aged 18–30
- College students
- Young professionals
- Skincare enthusiasts
- People interested in simple skincare routines
- Instagram users interested in beauty and self-care

---

💡 Customer Problem

The campaign focuses on common everyday skincare concerns such as:

- Skin feeling dry after washing the face
- Skin feeling tight or uncomfortable
- Dull-looking skin
- Difficulty maintaining a simple skincare routine
- Confusion about choosing an everyday moisturizer

The advertisements present GlowPure as a simple hydration step in an everyday skincare routine.

---

🎯 Campaign Objective

The main objectives of this UGC campaign are:

1. Create awareness about GlowPure.
2. Connect with the audience through relatable skincare problems.
3. Present the product naturally through UGC-style content.
4. Demonstrate how the product can fit into a daily routine.
5. Encourage viewers to explore or try the product.
6. Create reusable marketing content for Instagram.

---

✍️ Content Created

The final content pack includes:

- 10 UGC hooks
- 5 UGC video scripts
- 10 calls to action
- 5 Instagram captions
- 5 creative/video concepts
- Suggested on-screen text
- Structured AI prompts
- Product brief
- UGC advertisement strategy
- Instagram Reel concept
- Creative mockup ideas

---

🤖 AI & Prompt Engineering

AI was used as a creative assistant to generate and refine the advertising content.

The prompts were structured using:

1. Product information
2. Target audience
3. Customer problem
4. Product positioning
5. Brand personality
6. Content format
7. Platform requirements
8. Video duration
9. UGC style
10. CTA requirements
11. Advertising restrictions

The structured approach helped generate consistent content across multiple formats.

---

🧠 UGC Strategy

The advertisements follow a simple structure:

Problem → Product Introduction → Demonstration → Personal Experience → CTA

This structure helps capture attention quickly and guides the viewer toward the desired action.

The content was intentionally written in a conversational style so that it feels more like a creator recommendation than a traditional advertisement.

---

🎥 Video Concept

The main Instagram Reel follows a young woman discussing dry-feeling skin after washing her face.

The video includes:

Scene 1: Attention-grabbing hook
Scene 2: Product introduction
Scene 3: Product demonstration
Scene 4: Personal experience
Scene 5: Final product shot and CTA

Primary CTA

«Try GlowPure today.»
