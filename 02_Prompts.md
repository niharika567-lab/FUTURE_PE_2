Master UGC Advertisement Prompt

You are an expert UGC advertising copywriter and social media strategist.

Create a complete UGC advertising campaign for the following fictional product:

Brand: GlowPure

Product: GlowPure Hydrating Face Moisturizer

Category: Skincare

Target Audience:
Women aged 18–30, especially college students and young professionals.

Customer Problem:
The audience may experience dry, tight, uncomfortable, or dull-looking skin after washing their face and may want a simple skincare routine.

Product Positioning:
A lightweight everyday moisturizer designed to provide hydration and leave the skin feeling soft, comfortable, and fresh.

Brand Personality:
Friendly, youthful, natural, fresh, simple, and trustworthy.

Platform:
Instagram Reels.

Content Style:
Authentic, conversational, relatable UGC content that feels like a genuine creator recommendation rather than a traditional advertisement.

Create:

1. 10 attention-grabbing UGC hooks.
2. 5 UGC video scripts.
3. 10 persuasive CTAs.
4. 5 Instagram captions.
5. 5 video/creative concepts.
6. Suggested on-screen text.
7. Product demonstration ideas.
8. A short explanation of the customer problem addressed by each concept.

Requirements:

- Use simple conversational language.
- Make the first few seconds attention-grabbing.
- Keep the scripts suitable for 20–30 second videos.
- Make the content sound natural when spoken.
- Focus on the audience's everyday problem.
- Introduce the product naturally.
- Include a clear CTA.
- Avoid exaggerated claims.
- Avoid medical claims.
- Avoid guaranteed results.
- Avoid fake testimonials.
- Keep the content suitable for Instagram.

Hook Generation Prompt

Generate 10 short UGC hooks for GlowPure Hydrating Face Moisturizer.

The hooks should target women aged 18–30 and focus on the everyday problem of dry or uncomfortable-feeling skin.

Make each hook conversational, relatable, and suitable for the first 2–3 seconds of an Instagram Reel.

Avoid exaggerated claims and medical statements.

---

UGC Script Prompt

Create 5 different 20–30 second UGC scripts for GlowPure.

Use these five approaches:

1. Problem → Solution
2. Personal experience
3. Product demonstration
4. College/student routine
5. Honest creator-style recommendation

Each script should include:

- Hook
- Dialogue
- Visual direction
- Product demonstration
- On-screen text
- CTA

Keep the tone natural and authentic.

---

CTA Generation Prompt

Generate 10 short CTAs for GlowPure Hydrating Face Moisturizer.

The CTAs should be suitable for Instagram advertisements and should encourage viewers to explore or try the product without making unrealistic promises.

Caption Generation Prompt

Generate 5 Instagram captions for the GlowPure UGC campaign.

Each caption should:

- Introduce the product
- Address the customer problem
- Communicate the everyday hydration positioning
- Include a CTA
