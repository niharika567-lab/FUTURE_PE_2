🧴 GlowPure Product Brief

Brand Name

GlowPure

Product Name

GlowPure Hydrating Face Moisturizer

Category

Skincare

Product Type

Everyday facial moisturizer

Target Audience

Women aged 18–30, especially:

- College students
- Young professionals
- Skincare enthusiasts
- Social media users

Customer Problem

The target audience may experience skin that feels dry, tight, uncomfortable, or dull-looking after washing their face.

They may also want a simple skincare routine without too many complicated steps.

Product Positioning

GlowPure is positioned as a simple everyday hydration step for people who want a lightweight and easy-to-use moisturizer.

Key Product Benefit

The fictional product is positioned around providing lightweight hydration and leaving the skin feeling soft, comfortable, and fresh.

Brand Personality

- Friendly
- Youthful
- Natural
- Fresh
- Simple
- Trustworthy
- Approachable

Content Style

The advertising content should feel:

- Authentic
- Conversational
- Relatable
- Creator-generated
- Natural
- Social-media friendly

Primary Platform

Instagram Reels

Recommended Video Length

20–30 seconds

Campaign Goal

Increase product awareness and encourage viewers to explore or try the product.

Primary CTA

Try GlowPure today.

Advertising Restrictions

The campaign should avoid:

- Medical claims
- Guaranteed results
- Fake testimonials
- Unrealistic transformations
- Disease-treatment claims
- Misleading scientific claims

Campaign Message

Simple skincare. Everyday hydration.
